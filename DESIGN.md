# Design System: Hidup Kok Gini?

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Meja Main" (the games table)**

The screen is a board game spread out on a table, not an app that contains a board. Every element the player could conceivably pick up — tiles, buttons, cards, chips, pawns, the die, stickers, nameplates — is a flat field of saturated colour wearing a near-black outline (2–2.5px `--edge`) and a hard, zero-blur bottom edge in that same ink. That edge is the shadow a die casts on a table, not a UI elevation effect. The empty table around the board is not empty: authored SVG bits (a card fan, loose dice, meeples, a coin stack) sit scattered in the side bands at jaunty angles.

Density is high and deliberately unapologetic. Type runs small and heavy, colour runs loud, and the surfaces are cream paper by day (`#ffeccf`) and deep grape by lamplight (`#1a1030`). The printed pieces do not obey the lamp: the seven tile colours and their ink are declared once on `:root` and are never redefined for dark mode, so a pink tile is the same pink at midnight. What switches is the table, the ink, the lines, the scrim and the positive/negative chips.

The whole game lives inside one viewport. There is no page scroll and no visible scrollbar at any size; the board resizes itself through container queries and the sidebar becomes a bottom sheet on phones. This is the one constraint that outranks everything else visually — the app is played on a phone passed around a group, so anything that would push content off the bottom gets shrunk, dropped, or moved into internal scroll instead.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Flat saturated colour fields, never gradients or tints
- 2–2.5px near-black outline on every pick-up-able piece
- Hard zero-blur bottom edge (`0 Npx 0 -1px var(--edge)`) as the only elevation language
- Small pieces rotated 3–8°; structural panels stay square
- Seven printed piece colours that never change between themes
- One viewport, no page scroll, no visible scrollbars, at any size

## Colors

A sunny printed-cardboard palette: one hot tangerine for action, one sunny yellow for reward, and a seven-colour set of saturated printed pieces that sit on cream by day and grape by lamplight.

### Primary
- **Tangerine** (`--accent` #ff5d2e): every primary action. The roll button, the active-tile outline, the active-seat outline, the turn chip, the counters in the footer nav, the theme-toggle pressed state. Identical in both themes. Its text partner is **Burnt Cocoa** (`--accent-ink` #331704) on the fill, and **Chilli Red** (`--accent-text` #c4340a, #ffa072 in dark) for accent-coloured text on a paper ground.
- **Sunny Yellow** (`--fixed` #ffd23f): rewards, the active month token, the resolved medal, the winning result tab, the end-of-game badge, the brand mark, and the title halo (`--title-glow`, same value). Identical in both themes; its ink is always the deep grape `--fixed-ink` #2a1c3f.

### Secondary — the printed pieces
Seven token colours drive the board ring, the player seats, the stat meters, the category headers and the stickers. **These are declared only on `:root` and are never redefined for dark mode.**
- **Grape** (`--tile-1` #c08cf7), **Hot Pink** (`--tile-2` #ff7fae), **Sun** (`--tile-3` #ffd75e), **Sky** (`--tile-4` #62cdf5), **Tangerine Wash** (`--tile-5` #ffa06a), **Grass** (`--tile-6` #6ddc8b), **Lime** (`--tile-7` #a8e05f), plus **Wild Yellow** (`--tile-wild` #ffe14d) for START/GAJIAN.
- The seat `colors` in `src/data/content/players.json` draw the four seat colours from this same set (Sun, Sky, Hot Pink, Grass) — a seat colour is a piece colour, never a separate ramp.
- Ink on any piece colour is always **Deep Grape** (`--tile-ink` #2a1c3f), in both themes.

### Tertiary — verdict chips
- **Mint / Forest** (`--positive-bg` #d8f6dd on `--positive-ink` #1c6b35; #1e4a2c/#a8f0bd in dark): stat gains.
- **Blush / Brick** (`--negative-bg` #ffdfd6 on `--negative-ink` #b13312; #57221a/#ffb9a3 in dark): stat losses and the error notice.

### Neutral
- **Sunny Table** (`--page` #ffeccf; #1a1030 dark): the app ground, always carrying a 22px dot grid (`--dots`) so the table reads as a printed surface.
- **Card Paper / Raised / Field** (`--paper` #fffcf5, `--raised` #fff4e2, `--input` #fffdf8; #241640 / #2f1e55 / #2b1c4d dark): panels, secondary surfaces, and input wells.
- **Board Ink** (`--ink` #2a1c3f; #ffeede dark) for text, **Faded Ink** (`--muted` #6f5f86; #b6a3d4 dark) for supporting copy and labels.
- **Outline** (`--edge` #2a1c3f; #100a1f dark): every border and every hard shadow.
- **Rule Line** (`--line` #e8d7bb; #46326f dark): 1px dividers and dashed separators only — never a piece outline.
- **Focus** (`--focus` #4a2ddb light, #ffd23f dark): the 3px focus ring, deliberately off-palette so it cannot be mistaken for a game state.

### Named Rules
**The Printed Piece Rule.** `--tile-*` and `--tile-ink` are defined once, on `:root`, and are never redefined per theme. Printed pieces keep their colour when the lights go down. A new piece colour joins the seven-token set or it does not exist.

**The Reserved Meaning Rule.** Tangerine means "act now". Sunny yellow means "reward, active month, or win". A player's seat colour means "this turn belongs to this person". A piece colour means category. Never spend any of the four on decoration.

**The Two-Ink Rule.** Text is `--ink` or `--muted`, and text on any coloured piece is `--tile-ink`. There is no third grey.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Roboto (self-hosted variable, 100–900), with `sans-serif` fallback
**Body Font:** Roboto (same face)
**Label/Mono Font:** Roboto Mono (self-hosted, 400–700), with `ui-monospace, monospace`

**Character:** One family doing all the work at extreme weights. Headings sit at 900–950 with tight negative tracking so they read as printed box copy; body and labels sit small, plain and dense. Roboto is a pinned brand commitment from PRODUCT.md; Roboto Mono appears only where something is being counted. Roboto's ubiquity is a recorded, accepted trade (see `.impeccable/config.json`), not an unconsidered default.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (950, `clamp(24px, 10cqw, 64px)`, line-height 0.86, tracking −0.075em, rotated −3°): the board-centre title only. Carries a 3px/4px hard offset text-shadow in `--title-glow`.
- **Headline** (900, 24–41px responsive, line-height 1.07, tracking −1.1 to −1.5px): setup heading, drawn-card title, resolved verdict, results heading.
- **Title** (800, 21px desktop / 18px phone / 25px in dialogs, tracking −0.7px): the active player's name, dialog headings, brand lockup (23px/900).
- **Body** (400, 12px, line-height 1.6): card descriptions, dialog lead, rules list. 13px on phones for descriptions, choice labels, dialog lead and rules; 14px only above 1300×850.
- **Body Small** (400–650, 11px, line-height 1.5): choice labels, stat names, notes, log entries.
- **Label** (700–800, 10px, tracking 1–1.4px, usually uppercase, `--muted` or `--accent-text`): panel toplines, board toolbar, timeline label, field labels, footer meta.
- **Mono** (700, 10px, tracking 0.4–0.7px): tile numbers, panel code, card serial ("KARTU KEHIDUPAN"). Counting only.

### Named Rules
**The Shared-Phone Floor Rule.** No fixed pixel size is below 10px, and on phones (≤760px) anything a player has to *read to decide* is 12px or larger: card descriptions, choice labels, effect chips, stat values, the roll copy, the landing note, footer buttons and rules text. 10–11px is reserved for labels (uppercase, tracked), tile numbers and counters. The floor exists because the game is read by four people leaning over one phone. Never add a fixed 8px or 9px step. Printed board furniture (`tile-label*`, `sticker*`, `board-subtitle*`) holds 10px on phones; on a short ring the tile labels and stickers are dropped instead of shrunk.

**The One-Role-Many-Rungs Rule.** The ramp is larger than seven sizes because each heading role steps down across breakpoints and short screens instead of wrapping or scrolling: the setup heading runs 35 → 31 → 30 → 27 → 25px, the drawn-card title 33 → 28 → 26 → 25 → 23 → 22px, the verdict 32 → 29 → 28px, the board title across three container-query clamps. When a role needs a new rung, add it as a named token here rather than inventing a one-off value in CSS.

**The Mono-Counts-Only Rule.** Roboto Mono appears on tile numbers, panel codes and card serials. Prose, headings and buttons are never mono.

## Layout

The shell is a fixed-height column: `100dvh`, `max-width: 1600px`, 28px side gutters (18px ≤1000px, 14px ≤760px, 10px ≤390px), with `overflow: hidden` on `html`, `body` and `#root`. Header (72px), month strip, play area, footer (48px) — the play area takes the remaining space and nothing else is allowed to grow.

**Desktop (≥761px):** a two-column grid, board column plus a fixed rail (`minmax(0,1fr) 340px`; 370px above 1300×850, 310px below 1000px). The board panel is a container-query context (`container-type: size`); the 5×5 ring sizes itself as `width: min(100cqw, 112cqh)` / `height: min(89.286cqw, 100cqh)`, so the board always fits its box rather than its viewport. The centre 3×3 holds the title, illustration frame and stickers. The empty bands left and right of the ring carry the scattered `TableBits`, positioned in `cqw`-clamped percentages.

**Phone (≤760px):** the grid collapses to a column, the player dock and side sticker are dropped, and the control panel becomes a **bottom sheet** — absolutely positioned at the bottom of the play area, `max-height: calc(100% - 12px)`, over a `--scrim` backdrop, with a sticky topline and a close button. Closing it reveals a full-width reopen button. The desktop side bands do not exist at this width, so `TableBits` is hidden and `CenterBits` scatters a die, a card fan, coins and a meeple inside the board centre instead.

**Short viewports:** three further branches (`max-height: 760px` with `min-width: 761px`, `max-height: 680px` on phones, and `max-height: 500px`) compress header heights, shrink the dice stage, and progressively hide decorative copy — the intro line, the side sticker, the position note, the result medal. Decoration yields; controls never do.

**Spacing rhythm:** gaps and paddings cluster on 4 / 6 / 9 / 12 / 14 / 18 / 21 / 28px, tightening roughly one step per breakpoint. It is a rhythm, not a strict multiple-of-8 scale.

### Named Rules
**The One Viewport Rule.** The app never scrolls the page and never shows a scrollbar. `overflow: hidden` on the document, `scrollbar-width: none` globally, and internal `overflow: auto` with `overscroll-behavior: contain` on the control panel, dialog and bottom sheet. If new content does not fit, shrink it, drop it, or put it inside a panel that scrolls — never let the page grow.

**The Board Measures Itself Rule.** Board sizing is expressed in container query units against `.board-arena`, never in viewport units. A new board-area element sizes in `cqw`/`cqh` so it survives every rail width.

## Elevation & Depth

There is exactly one elevation device: a **hard, zero-blur bottom edge** in the outline ink, `box-shadow: 0 Npx 0 -1px var(--edge)`, where N scales with how big the piece is. It is a printed object sitting on a table, not a light source. Combined with the 2–2.5px outline, it is the whole depth vocabulary; there are no ambient shadows, no blurs and no tinted glows on any piece.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Tiny piece** (`0 2px 0 -0.5px var(--edge)`): the name avatar and the small die.
- **Piece** (`0 3px 0 -1px var(--edge)`): board tiles, chips, pawns, choice buttons, seat cards, payday receipt.
- **Card** (`0 4px 0 -1px var(--edge)`): brand mark, stickers, timeline, print frame, medals, player cards.
- **Primary** (`0 5px 0 -1px var(--edge)`): the primary button and the drawn event card.
- **Panel** (`0 6px 0 -1px var(--edge)`): board panel, control panel, the big die.
- **Dialog** (`0 8px 0 -1px var(--edge)`): the modal.
- **Press response:** hover deepens by 2px (`0 7px`) with `translateY(-2px)`; active flattens to `0 1px` with `translateY(3px)` — the piece is pressed into the table.
- **Cast shadow (objects only):** `.dice-shadow` is a blurred ellipse in `--dice-shadow` under the die stage. This is a *depicted* shadow of a physical die, not a UI elevation, and it is the only blur allowed on a piece.
- **Scrim:** `--scrim` over the board behind the bottom sheet; the dialog backdrop is `#1a1030b8` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

### Named Rules
**The Hard Edge Rule.** Elevation is a hard offset edge in `--edge` with zero blur and zero spread beyond `-1px`. No soft UI shadow, anywhere, at any breakpoint. If a piece needs to feel higher, increase the offset — never add blur.

**The Outline-First Rule.** A piece is legible from its 2–2.5px `--edge` outline alone. The bottom edge adds weight; it never carries the shape by itself.

## Shapes

Flat fields with a consistent outline and a family of soft-but-modest corners. Radii run 4–6px on the smallest board furniture (tiles on phone, center stickers, the small inline dice), 7–9px on chips, tiles, avatars and inputs, 10–12px on choices, the art frame, the ending card and wide-desktop tiles, 14–16px on cards, panels and the big die, 18px on the dialog (15px on phone), and a full 20px pill on every chip and badge. Phones tighten panels to 8px and tiles to 4px so the ring keeps its grid legibility at small sizes.

Two silhouettes are specific to the world: the **pawn**, a dome (`border-radius: 48% 48% 35% 35%`, 35×40px, 2.5px outline, with a curved mouth drawn from a bottom border), and the **square die**, a 72px rounded square (16px radius, 3px outline) holding a 3×3 CSS pip grid.

Rotation is the tell of a loose piece. Small pieces sit at **3–8°** (brand mark −8°, current month token −7° with 1.1 scale, stickers ±3°, name avatar −5°, turn chip 3°, medals −8°, the big die −8°, the title −3°); the scattered table bits go further, 6–22°. Structural panels, the board grid, the rail and the footer stay perfectly square.

### Named Rules
**The Loose Piece Rule.** If you can pick it up, tilt it 3–8°. If it holds other things — panel, grid, header, sheet — it stays at 0°.

### Motion
- **Dice roll** (`dice-roll`, 0.24s linear infinite): −12° → 168° with a −6px lift → 348°, running only while the roll is in flight. The result is committed to state before the animation starts.
- **Pawn hop** (`pawn-hop`, 210ms `cubic-bezier(0.2,0.6,0.4,1)`): squash to (1.05, 0.85), a −17px arc at −9° with a stretch, land with a squash. Played once per tile, tile by tile, so movement is countable.
- **Card drop** (`card-drop`, 0.48s `cubic-bezier(0.2,0.7,0.2,1)`, origin top centre): the card falls from −25px at −7°, overshoots to +5px at 2°, settles square.
- **Result pop** (`result-pop`, 0.25–0.3s ease-out) and **dialog in** (`dialog-in`, 0.24s ease-out): a short rise from 10–18px with a fade.
- **Interaction:** buttons transition `transform` and `background` at 0.16s; choice buttons add `box-shadow` at 0.15s; stat meters scale over 0.3s.
- **Reduced motion:** `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` kills all animation, transition and smooth scrolling globally with `!important`. Motion is presentation only; nothing about the game depends on it.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** give every pick-up-able element a flat colour field, a 2–2.5px `--edge` outline, and a hard bottom edge from the Shadow Vocabulary (`0 3px` piece, `0 4px` card, `0 5px` primary, `0 6px` panel).
- **Do** pull new piece colours from the seven `--tile-*` tokens and leave them undefined in dark mode.
- **Do** tilt loose pieces 3–8° and keep structural panels at 0°.
- **Do** keep tangerine for actions, sunny yellow for rewards and the active month, and the seat colour for turn ownership.
- **Do** size anything inside the board arena in container query units (`cqw`/`cqh`).
- **Do** hold the 10px type floor and reach for 11–12px body before shrinking a label.
- **Do** put overflow inside a panel with `overflow: auto` and `overscroll-behavior: contain`, and let the page stay fixed at `100dvh`.
- **Do** put Roboto Mono only on things that are counted: tile numbers, panel codes, card serials.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a blurred or ambient UI shadow to any piece or panel. The only blur in the system is the die's depicted cast shadow and the dialog backdrop.
- **Don't** redefine `--tile-*` or `--tile-ink` under `[data-theme="dark"]`.
- **Don't** use gradients, tints, or opacity-faded fills to make a new colour. If it is not a token, it does not exist.
- **Don't** let the page scroll or a scrollbar appear at any viewport size.
- **Don't** declare a font size below 10px.
- **Don't** put `--line` around a piece; it is for 1px dividers only, and `--edge` is for pieces.
- **Don't** rotate a panel, the board grid, the header or the footer.
- **Don't** spend tangerine or sunny yellow on decoration — both are reserved meanings.

## Motion pass — September 2026

Every interaction now answers back, and the motion is made of the table's own materials: flat cardboard bits with an `--edge` outline, hard-edged stamps and coins, never a blur or a glow. Keyframes and overrides live in `src/styles/motion.css` (loaded after the component styles); flying pieces are drawn by `src/motion/fx.ts` into a fixed `.fx-layer`, or inside the open `<dialog>` so they show above the top layer.

- **The focal moment: the card turns over.** A drawn card is dealt face down in its category colour (the crossed-stripe card back), flips on the Y axis over 700ms, and the face appears at 40%. As the face lands, a burst of category-coloured bits fires from the category bar, the title punches in, and the choices are dealt in from the right, 90ms apart, with their effect chips popping after them.
- **Choosing is a stamp, not a click.** The tapped choice squashes and a tangerine **DIPILIH** stamp slams onto it while the other choices slide off the table; the game receives the choice 460ms later. Taps during the stamp are ignored. The payday slip uses the same stamp.
- **The camera follows the pawn.** A move runs through five stages in `useTurnMotion`: *rolling* (480ms), *focus* (450ms, the board zooms to 1.7× onto the pawn's tile before it moves), *moving* (450ms per hop, the camera pans so the pawn's tile stays centred), *landing* (850ms, the last hop), and *settling* (550ms, the camera pulls back to the whole board). The card is dealt only after settling. The camera is a translate + scale on `.board-grid` (`boardMotionStyle`), clipped by the arena and, as a safety net, by the panel.
- **The last hop is heavy.** The pawn crouches, rises higher and slower, and slams down at 62% with a flat squash, a wobble, a tile dip, a camera thud, dust, an outlined shockwave and a table jolt. Hop heights come from the board's size (`--hop-lift: clamp(30px, 12cqh, 90px)`, `--land-lift: clamp(52px, 20cqh, 150px)`), so a wide-screen board gets bigger jumps.
- **The die lands with weight.** In the air it tumbles and its face flickers; on landing it slams and squashes, two outlined rings (tangerine, then yellow) shock out, and the board panel jolts for 380ms.
- **Stats count.** A changed stat rolls to its new value over 650ms, bumps in `--positive-ink` or flinches in `--negative-ink`, and floats a +/− chip in the verdict colours. A timer settles the true value even when the tab is not painting.
- **Rewards rain.** A positive payday drops flat yellow coins over the dialog; a negative one shakes the slip instead. The net total slams onto the slip before its MASUK/NOMBOK stamp drops. The end of the year rains confetti twice.
- **Feedback everywhere.** Primary buttons squash on press and their arrow slides on hover; the roll button taps the table every few seconds while it is waiting; icon buttons tilt; every action button throws a pinch of confetti from the pointer. Dialogs pop in with a slight overshoot and a fading backdrop. The active player's seat swaps in from the left and the dock seat hops. The theme toggle sweeps the new lighting out from the button as a circle (View Transitions, where supported).
- **Reduced motion** still turns every CSS animation off, every `fx.ts` effect is a no-op, and choices commit immediately without the stamp. Colour changes that confirm a stat change remain.
